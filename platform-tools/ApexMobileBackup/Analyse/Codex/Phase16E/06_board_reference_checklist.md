# Board-reference checklist

The three archived local images were loaded and compared. This checklist is
for future visual identity confirmation only. It is not authorization or an
instruction to open the phone or make electrical contact.

| Landmark | Pinout close-up | Full-device demonstration | Second board demonstration | Future phone check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Handset orientation | Mainboard lower edge above orange flex | Camera at handset top, battery below mainboard | Camera and mainboard top visible | Confirm camera/top and USB/bottom orientation before using any close-up |
| USB connector | Not visible | Bottom daughterboard not visible | Not visible | Confirm USB is at handset bottom; do not infer point orientation from a cropped image alone |
| Shield geometry | Large central shield with stepped lower edge | Same central shield geometry | Same shield and edge profile | Must match before any contact |
| Battery area | Orange flex and battery edge below board | Battery clearly below mainboard | Orange flex below mainboard | Battery placement and connector route must agree |
| Camera neighborhood | Outside the tight crop | Rear camera at upper-left of the opened handset | Rear camera at upper-left | Must agree with reference orientation |
| Testpoint neighborhood | Large circular exposed pad below central shield, beside a short pad row and screw | Probe shown in the same lower-mainboard region | Probe, shield, screw, pad row, and board marking visible together | Exact geometry must match all landmarks, not only one circular pad |
| Silkscreen | `HL2PRAM ... RF VER.D` visible | Too distant for reliable reading | `HL2PRAM2 RF VER.D -HF` visible | Record actual revision photograph and compare before proceeding |

Required future physical tools: plastic opening picks, suitable suction tool,
small precision screwdriver set, ESD-safe workspace, and a nonconductive
spudger. Metal tools must not be used near an energized exposed board unless a
later explicitly authorized procedure proves they are necessary and defines a
safe boundary.

The actual PRA-LX1 has not been opened or photographed internally. A future
board comparison must stop on any revision, shield, screw, connector, pad-row,
or orientation mismatch.
