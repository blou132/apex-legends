## Executive result

Phase16Q precisely bounded `DolphinUpdater::CheckUpdate` with ELF unwind
metadata: `0x05a2eb00..0x05a2fdbc` (end exclusive). The first `0x500` bytes are
a mixed opaque prefix; a coherent 879-word AArch64 continuation begins at ELF
`0x05a2f000`. A direct-edge backward CFG from the real interface load reaches
that address and stops because no reliable predecessor exists.

The continuation confirms `X19` as the owner register, but its source remains
in the opaque prefix. It also resolves `W23` to a local helper-result predicate
and proves owner `+0x45` is persistent state: first-extract success clears it,
while two `CheckUpdate` paths set it to one.

The exact callback initializer has zero direct code callers. Therefore callback
placement, callback `+0x08` source, and the enclosing owner initializer cannot
be recovered from that axis. In the exact `CheckUpdate` FDE and already proven
owner lifecycle methods, the only `+0x1f0` store remains the `Shutdown` clear
at ELF `0x04712ee0`. No non-null writer or acquisition call is proven.

Owner `+0x1f8` has three exact readers and one clear. Its virtual cleanup and
use as Init `X5` support a probable related-interface role, without proving its
exact type. The primary `+0x1f0` acquisition source cannot be connected to
`GCloudDolphinImp`; ABI compatibility alone is insufficient.
