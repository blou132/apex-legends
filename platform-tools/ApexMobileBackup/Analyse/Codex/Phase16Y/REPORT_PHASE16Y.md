# Phase16Y - Unreal reflection owner-type audit

## Executive result

Phase16Y independently confirms a standalone
`/Script/PureClient.DolphinUpdater` UClass. Its primary lazy accessor is
Ghidra `0x081b12a0` (ELF `0x080b12a0`), its UClass cell is Ghidra
`0x0b7bebe0`, and its registration reports size `0x430` with alignment
`0x08`. That size is compatible with the already-proven fields at `+0x1f0`
and `+0x1f8`.

The known `GameUpdateMgr` accessor is revalidated at Ghidra `0x081b1348`.
Its exact superclass resolves to `/Script/Basic.ApexGameInstanceSubsystem`.
The bounded compiled-in table contains adjacent primary entries for
`DolphinUpdater` and `GameUpdateMgr`, which validates the class-registration
model.

The registration ABI used here does not directly expose an `FClassParams`,
property pointer array, or class-scoped native function table. Both classes'
native-registration callbacks and constructor callbacks are non-decodable or
opaque in the preserved project. An earlier helper attempted to decompile
them; its bad-data output is excluded from the conclusions.

Consequently `GameUpdateMgr+0x38` cannot be proven reflected, its
PropertyClass cannot be compared with the recovered DolphinUpdater UClass,
and no reflected setter or non-null owner producer is available. The
standalone class existence and layout are confirmed, but the identity of the
specific object stored at `+0x38` remains probable. The authorized reflection
owner axis is exhausted within the authorized evidence boundary. This does
not prove that property metadata is absent elsewhere in the binary.

Review found a scope deviation in the earlier helper execution: common
package references caused surplus function exports, the shared registration
helper was exported/decompiled, and opaque callbacks were submitted to the
decompiler. [30_boundary.md](30_boundary.md) records this explicitly. Those
surplus results are local-only and excluded from the accepted evidence.

The cleaned supporting exports are [class_accessor.json](output/class_accessor.json),
[class_registration.json](output/class_registration.json),
[dolphinupdater_class.json](output/dolphinupdater_class.json), and
[gate.json](output/gate.json). They distinguish confirmed class existence
from the unproven owner type and retain evidence provenance.
