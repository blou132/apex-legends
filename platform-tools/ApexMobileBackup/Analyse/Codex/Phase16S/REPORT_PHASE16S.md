# Phase16S - DolphinUpdater class lifecycle

Phase16S used the exact preserved UE4 image and read-only Ghidra projects to
test the owner-class/vtable route without touching a device or running Apex.

An exact pointer scan of `.data.rel.ro`, `.rodata`, and `.data` found one cell
for the known DolphinUpdater::Shutdown method and none for the other three
known owner methods. No `0x200`-byte window therefore contained the required
two known methods. The Shutdown cell lies in dense stripped C++ dispatch
material, but its table start, RTTI, class ownership, and virtual slot remain
unresolved. Eight preceding and two following cells contain only generic
stubs and one external thunk.

Because a concrete DolphinUpdater vtable was not recovered, no exact vptr
writer, owner initializer, construction fields, allocation source,
pre-CheckUpdate lifecycle, or non-null `+0x1f0` acquisition write could be
proven. The static stop rule prevented broader scans.

The Dolphin-only dynamic-symbol audit found 29 symbol records across UE4 and
gcloud, representing 19 canonical entries after aliases. CreateDolphin is not
a new route: its previously known UE4 caller is dead/unreachable and was not
reopened. Helpers and release functions do not provide acquisition, and no
named symbol converges with a proven owner lifecycle method.
