# Phase16U - DolphinUpdater address-point writer provenance

Phase16U completed the minimal left extension omitted by Phase16T. ELF
`0x0ae565f8` is zero offset-to-top, `0x0ae56600` is zero typeinfo, and
`0x0ae56608` is the first relocated executable entry. The probable primary
address point is therefore confirmed by both the ELF parser and read-only
Ghidra.

No ELF relocation resolves into the exact vtable group, and no allocated data
payload contains an exact raw primary or secondary address point. As a
result, there is no alias cluster, VTT, or construction-vtable candidate.
Ghidra independently confirms the zero relocation-alias result.

Eight `.text` instructions target the group's page. Bounded register-aware
dataflow and Ghidra disassembly show that all eight form addresses outside the
DolphinUpdater group. No exact literal load, GOT/data route, indirect address
point construction, or vptr store remains. The constructor and its fields are
therefore still unknown, and the static address-point writer axis is
exhausted under the authorized boundary.
